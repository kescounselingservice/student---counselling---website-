KES Counselling Service — Overview 🎓 What is KES Counselling Service? KES Counselling Service is a student-support initiative designed to provide students with a safe, supportive and confidential space where they can talk about their emotions, personal challenges, academic pressure, relationships, stress and other difficulties. The purpose is to make students feel heard, understood, supported and guided — without fear of judgment. 💙 What the service aims to provide Individual counselling sessions — private conversations with a counsellor. Session booking — students can request appointments. Session rescheduling — students can request a change in their appointment. Two counsellors — students can choose or connect with the appropriate counsellor. Student support — a place to talk about academic stress, personal difficulties and emotional wellbeing. Polls — counsellors can create polls and students can participate. Student profile — students can manage their profile and account. Counsellor dashboard — counsellors can manage student information, sessions, appointments and related activities. Weekly analytics — charts to track sessions conducted throughout the week. 🌱 The vision Every student deserves someone who listens, a space where they feel safe, and support when life becomes difficult. The service is not about judging students or telling them what they are doing wrong. It's about helping them understand themselves, navigate their challenges, build confidence and move forward.
